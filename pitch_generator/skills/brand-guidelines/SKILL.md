# Brand Guidelines

## Palette
- Primary: Deep indigo and slate
- Accents: Warm amber and terracotta

## Light
- Warm raking golden-hour light with long soft shadows
- High contrast with natural falloff

## Composition
- Off-center hero subject with generous negative space
- Shallow depth of field with cinematic focus

## Subject
- One realistic photographic subject
- Authentic, handcrafted materials and textures

## Never
- No neon or fluorescent cyan/magenta colors
- No watermarks, text overlays, or logos
- No ring light or artificial flat studio lighting
- No 3D renders or CGI aesthetics
